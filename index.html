Programme

Four days, one public expo

60-minute keynotes, 30-minute peer-reviewed talks in two parallel tracks, and every day finishes by 5 pm.

TUE 7 DEC · OPEN TO ALL

Public Space Expo

Opening keynote, 25 exhibition stands, start-up pitch stage, schools programme and the space tourism experience.

WED 8 DEC

Technology & Innovation

Vehicles and spaceports, passenger safety and training, and an expert panel.

THU 9 DEC

Workshops & Investment

Medical readiness, spaceport operations, space law and insurance, financing – plus the investor roundtable.

FRI 10 DEC

Sustainability & Regulation

Space debris, launch emissions and the rules of the road. Closing keynote and awards.
